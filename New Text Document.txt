From the sins of their servants,
A boundary, too thick to shatter their own,
A tapestry, too warped to see the world,
A space, too vain to see anything beyond coin,
A sky, too weak to not be swept by the abyss.

To the mistakes of their masters,
A moon, too gloomy to ever see beyond the despair,
A sun, too violent to ever grow anything but flames,
A world, too indifferent to ever raise and care.

And yet, there is still darkness in the divine,
An ocean, too impulsive to not bring judgement,
Its rage turns everything to ruins,
A forest, too ignorant to see the impending apocalypse,
Its foolishness scorching the soil.

Woe those who split their will. 
Woe those who never try to stand their stand.
Woe to me, the crimson heart waiting at world's end,
Who tried to hold ideals through hardships,
but the reality of consequences has shattered it.
